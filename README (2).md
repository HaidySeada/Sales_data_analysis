# Executive Sales & Performance Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, revenue generation, order volume, product category performance, geographic sales distribution, sales representative contributions, and customer payment preferences.

The dashboard transforms sales transaction data into actionable business insights through data cleaning, data transformation, data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive data visualization. It supports executive decision-making by highlighting sales trends, identifying high-performing products and regions, and evaluating sales team performance.

## Objectives

- Monitor key sales KPIs, including total revenue, order volume, and units sold.
- Analyze revenue contribution across product categories.
- Compare sales performance across geographic markets.
- Evaluate sales representative performance and revenue contribution.
- Examine customer payment method preferences.
- Identify monthly sales trends and fluctuations over time.
- Discover opportunities to improve revenue growth and sales performance.
- Support strategic decisions through interactive reporting and data-driven insights.

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Loading:** Import and inspect the available sales transaction dataset.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Identify and address missing values, duplicates, inconsistent categories, and data type issues.
3. **Data Transformation:** Use Power Query to clean, standardize, and prepare the data for analysis.
4. **Data Modeling:** Organize relevant tables and establish relationships to support accurate reporting.
5. **KPI Development:** Create DAX measures to calculate revenue, order volume, units sold, and other relevant sales metrics.
6. **Product Analysis:** Evaluate sales performance and revenue contribution across product categories.
7. **Geographic Analysis:** Compare sales performance across cities and markets.
8. **Sales Team Analysis:** Assess individual sales representative contributions and performance differences.
9. **Payment Analysis:** Explore transaction distribution across credit cards, cash, and gift cards.
10. **Trend Analysis:** Analyze monthly sales patterns and fluctuations over time.
11. **Dashboard Development:** Build an interactive executive dashboard with dynamic filters and visualizations.
12. **Insights & Recommendations:** Translate analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.

## Repository Setup & Privacy

To protect confidential transaction records, the raw Power BI `.pbix` file and underlying business data may be excluded from the public repository.

- **Dashboard Preview:** Provides a visual overview of the report.
- **Power BI Template (`.pbit`):** Can be shared to demonstrate the report layout, model structure, and calculations, subject to a review of embedded metadata and data-source information.
- **Interactive Demo:** A Power BI Service link can be provided if the report is published and approved for public access.

A `.pbit` template does not automatically guarantee that all sensitive information has been removed. Review the model, queries, parameters, and metadata before sharing.
